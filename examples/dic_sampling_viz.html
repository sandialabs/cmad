Face is the zmax side of the (1, 1, 0.1) plate (unit square). Meshes are drawn as structured triangulations at the chosen element size to judge resolution and notch coverage; the real mesh is unstructured tets at the same h (via notch_mesh.py). Load ramps on the top edge (reaction = synthetic load cell); captured points become the DIC displacement cloud. Stereo DIC is assumed, so all three components are recorded.
